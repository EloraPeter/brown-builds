Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to Brown Builds. Discover how we collect, use, and protect your personal data.

Learn Your Rights

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Brown Builds, we prioritize your privacy and are committed to safeguarding your personal data in compliance with applicable privacy laws. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights to manage your information.

Who We Are

Brown Builds (“we,” “us”) operates this website and is responsible for collecting and managing your personal data in accordance with global privacy regulations, including GDPR and CCPA where applicable.

What Data We Collect

Personal Data You Provide

Includes your name, email, phone number, and location when you sign up, request a quote, or contact us.

Automatically Collected Data

Includes browsing data, IP addresses, device type, and browser information collected via cookies and analytics tools.

Third-Party Data

Collected from services like social media or payment processors, only with your consent and when you use these services.

How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Provide and enhance our services, such as processing quote requests or project inquiries.
  • Communicate with you, including sending updates or marketing materials (if you’ve opted in).
  • Process transactions and issue receipts for services.
  • Ensure legal compliance and protect site security.

Legal Basis

We process your data based on:

  • Consent: For cookies, marketing communications, or optional data collection.
  • Contract Performance: To fulfill services like quote requests or project contracts.
  • Legitimate Interests: For site security, analytics, and improving user experience.

Sharing Your Data

We may share your data with:

  • Service Providers: Such as payment gateways or email platforms to facilitate services.
  • Legal Recipients: When required by law or to protect our rights and safety.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined (e.g., completing transactions or meeting legal obligations). Data is securely deleted or anonymized when no longer needed.

Your Data Rights

Depending on your region, you may have the right to:

Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

Update or rectify inaccurate personal information.

Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal obligations.

Opt out of marketing communications or cookie usage.

Restrict certain uses of your data, such as for marketing.

Receive your data in a portable format, where applicable.

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@brownbuilds.com.

Data Security

We implement secure protocols (e.g., TLS/HTTPS) and limit internal access to your data. While we strive to protect your information, no online method is completely secure.

Children’s Privacy

Our website is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect or process data from children.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy to reflect changes in practices or regulations. Significant changes will be communicated via a website notice or updated effective date. Last updated: July 15, 2025.

Contact Us

Questions about our privacy practices? Reach out to us at info@brownbuilds.com or visit our contact page.
