Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We’ve Got Answers!

1. What services does Brown Builds offer?

We specialize in residential and commercial construction, building renovations, structural repairs, remodeling, finishing, and property maintenance. Whether you're building from scratch or upgrading an existing space, we've got you covered.

2. How do I request a quote?

You can request a free quote by filling out the form on our Contact page or calling us directly. We’ll schedule a site inspection or ask for relevant project details before giving an accurate estimate.

3. Is there a consultation fee?

Initial consultations and quotes are completely free. We only charge for more in-depth architectural planning or project drawings if required.

4. How long will my project take?

Project timelines depend on the scope, size, and complexity of the work. We'll give you an estimated timeline after the site inspection and keep you updated every step of the way.

5. Do you handle permits and approvals?

Yes, we assist clients in obtaining necessary building permits and regulatory approvals when required. If you already have approvals in place, we can also work directly with them.

6. Do you provide materials or should I supply my own?

We offer both options. We can handle full procurement and ensure you get quality, cost-effective materials. If you prefer to supply your own, we’ll advise on what’s best suited for the project.

7. Is your work guaranteed?

Absolutely. We offer a warranty on all workmanship and ensure that materials used meet industry standards. Specific warranty terms are discussed in the project contract.

8. Can I make changes after work has started?

Yes, but major changes may affect the timeline and cost. We’re flexible and happy to work with your evolving vision — all adjustments will be clearly communicated.

9. How do I know I’m getting quality service?

We have a portfolio of completed projects and a growing list of satisfied clients. Our team is made up of experienced builders, project managers, and engineers committed to excellence.

10. Do you offer post-construction maintenance?

Yes! We offer maintenance packages for residential and commercial properties — from plumbing and electrical to painting and touch-ups.

11. What locations do you serve?

We are based in Delta State, Nigeria, and serve surrounding cities and states. For larger projects, we’re open to travel across Nigeria.
