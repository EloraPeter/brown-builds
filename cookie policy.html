Our Cookie Policy

At Brown Builds, we use cookies and similar technologies to provide a seamless and personalized experience. This policy outlines how we use cookies, the types we employ, and how you can manage your preferences.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They enable us to recognize your device, remember your preferences, and analyze how you interact with our site to improve your experience.

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential for website functionality, such as enabling login sessions and navigation. These cannot be disabled.

Track visits across websites to deliver targeted advertisements and personalized content.

Third-Party Cookies

We partner with third-party services, such as analytics providers and social media platforms, which may place cookies on our site to improve functionality and track user interactions. These cookies are subject to the respective third-party privacy policies.

Manage Your Cookie Preferences

You can control cookies through your browser settings or our cookie consent popup. Disabling certain cookies may impact site functionality. Visit your browser’s help section for instructions on managing cookies.

Changes to Our Cookie Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in technology or legal requirements. Significant updates will be communicated via a notice on our website. Please review this page periodically for the latest information.
